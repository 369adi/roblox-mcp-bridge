# Studded model scale and geometry guide

Use these as rough starting assumptions only; confirm the requested scale and current helper conventions.
- Avatar: roughly 5 studs tall, about 2 studs wide.
- Door: around 4 studs wide by 7 studs tall.
- Storey: around 12–14 studs.
- Car: roughly 8 wide × 5 tall × 16 long.
- Y is vertical; Parts are positioned by their centre.
- Cylinders have their long axis along local X; upright cylinders need rotation.
- Build silhouette first, then secondary shapes, then a small number of accents.
- Hollow buildings require floor, walls with real openings, interior space and roof.
- Avoid coplanar faces; overlap seams slightly where appropriate.
- For moving assemblies, define a root and joints; do not unanchor a decorative model indiscriminately.
